MY PROJECT

1.NEXORA-FUTURE GAMING

Nexora is a modern gaming e-commerce website designed to bring gamers closer to their ultimate gaming experience. It offers a stylish and immersive platform where users can explore gaming products, discover the latest accessories, and experience a futuristic digital shopping environment. Built using HTML5 and CSS3, Nexora focuses on creative layouts, smooth animations, interactive hover effects, and responsive design. Inspired by mythical gaming worlds and neon aesthetics, the website combines creativity with modern web technologies. My goal in developing Nexora was to transform a simple shopping website into a unique, engaging, and visually appealing gaming destination.

2.CityZen – A Smart City Utility App

CityZen is a smart city utility app designed to make urban living easier, smarter, and more convenient. The main goal of this project is to connect citizens with essential city services through a simple and user-friendly digital platform. It focuses on improving accessibility, simplifying daily tasks, and providing a seamless user experience. The design includes intuitive navigation, clean layouts, and visually appealing interfaces to help users access important information and services effortlessly. This project helped me enhance my UI/UX design skills, understand user-centered design principles, and create meaningful digital experiences using Figma.
